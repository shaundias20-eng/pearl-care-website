Cookie Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Pearl Care Training Academy uses cookies and similar technologies on our website and learning platform.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites work and remember your session. We keep our use of cookies to the minimum needed to run the service.

Cookies we use

  • Essential cookies – required for the site and platform to function, including keeping you securely signed in to your learner account during a session. These cannot be switched off without breaking core features.
  • Preference cookies – remember basic choices, such as display settings, to improve your experience.

What we do not use

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not sell data collected through cookies.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may prevent parts of the platform, such as signing in, from working correctly.

For any question about cookies, you can contact us at hello@pearlcaretraining.com.
