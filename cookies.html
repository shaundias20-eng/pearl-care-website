Cookie Policy

Last updated 18 September 2026

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. This policy explains how cookies are used on the Pearl Care Training Academy website (pearlcaretraining.co.uk).

Our approach

We keep this simple. This website uses only strictly necessary cookies. We do not use analytics, advertising, social media or tracking cookies, and we do not profile visitors. Because we set no non-essential cookies, no cookie consent banner is required, and there is nothing you need to accept.

Cookies we may use

Strictly necessary cookies are ones that are essential for the site to work and to keep it secure. Any cookies set fall into this category and may include the following, placed by our hosting provider:

CookiePurposeType
Host security / anti-spamSet by our host (Netlify) to protect the enquiry form from automated abuse and to deliver the site securelyStrictly necessary

These cookies do not track you across other websites and are not used for advertising.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Because this site relies only on strictly necessary cookies, blocking them may affect how parts of the site function, but you will not miss any tracking or advertising features because we do not use any.

Changes

If we ever introduce analytics or other non-essential cookies in future, we will update this policy and add a consent banner that blocks those cookies until you accept them. The date at the top shows when this policy was last updated.
