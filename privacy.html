Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

Pearl Care Training Academy ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your rights under UK data protection law, including the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

Pearl Care Training Academy is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, you can contact us at hello@pearlcaretraining.com.

What we collect

  • Enquiry details – when you use our contact form we collect your name, email address, organisation (if provided) and the content of your message.
  • Learner account data – for users of our learning platform we hold account details (name, work email), course progress, assessment results and any competency records or certificates issued.
  • Technical data – basic information such as browser type and pages visited, used to keep the site secure and working correctly.

How we use your data

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide the training services you or your employer request.
  • To create and manage learner accounts, track progress and issue certificates and competency records.
  • To maintain the security, integrity and performance of our platform.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

Lawful bases

We process personal data on the bases of performance of a contract (delivering training), our legitimate interests (running and securing our service and responding to enquiries), consent (where you submit an enquiry), and compliance with legal obligations.

Sharing and processors

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with trusted service providers who process it on our behalf under appropriate agreements, including our hosting and database provider, our website host and form handler, and our video content provider. These providers act only on our instructions.

Retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above, to comply with legal obligations, and to maintain training and compliance records that learners and their employers may need for regulatory evidence. When data is no longer needed it is securely deleted.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of these rights, you can contact us at hello@pearlcaretraining.com. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

Our use of cookies is described in our Cookie Policy.

This policy is provided in good faith and reflects our current practices. If you have questions about how your data is handled, please get in touch.
